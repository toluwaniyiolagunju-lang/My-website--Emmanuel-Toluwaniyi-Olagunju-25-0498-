Page Formatting

Page formatting is a crucial aspect of web development that involves structuring and styling HTML documents to create visually appealing and well-organized web pages.

HTML Structure

The foundation of any web page is its HTML structure. This includes the <!DOCTYPE html> declaration, <html>, <head>, and <body> elements. The <head> section contains metadata, while the <body> holds the visible content.

Headings and Paragraphs

Headings (<h1> to <h6>) provide hierarchy and structure to your content. They range from the most important <h1> to less significant <h6>. Paragraphs (<p>) are used to group related sentences and ideas together.

Line Breaks and Horizontal Rules

Line breaks (<br>) create space within paragraphs, while horizontal rules (<hr>) visually separate sections of content. These elements help improve readability and organization.
